# Readings for Japanese lyrics

`ja-readings.deflate` is a table of Japanese words and their readings, one
`word<TAB>reading` per line (the reading left out for words written in
kana), sorted by the word's UTF-8 bytes, compressed as raw DEFLATE. The app
reads it to put readings over Japanese lyrics in the full-window player.

It is made from **JMdict**, the Japanese-Multilingual Dictionary, by the
[Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/)
(EDRDG), used under the group's
[licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. This table is shared under the same licence.

Kept: the written forms marked common, every word of one or two kanji, and
common words written in kana, each with the reading JMdict lists first for
that form.
